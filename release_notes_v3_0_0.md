# Becoming Eve — Release v3.0.0: The Character Creation & Dynamic Layered Wardrobe Update 💖👗

## Major Highlights & Features in v3.0.0

### 1. Full Character Creation & Styling Studio
- **Custom Name & Pronouns**: Choose your chosen name (default: "Eve") and pronouns (`She/Her`, `She/They`, `They/Them`, `Fae/Faer`, or Custom), reflected across every scene, speaker nameplate, mirror reflection, and HerSpace phone notifications.
- **Complexion Palette**: Choose from a spectrum of realistic skin undertones (Porcelain Ivory, Fair Peach, Warm Honey Beige, Golden Olive, Caramel Bronze, Deep Chestnut, Rich Espresso).
- **Physical Silhouettes**: 4 distinct silhouettes (Slender & Lithe, Soft Curves, Full Curves, Toned Athletic).
- **Nuances & Details**: Delicate beauty mark toggle and optional faint early stubble shadow for authentic early boy-mode exploration in Era 0.
- **Hair Salon**: Multiple hair cuts (Boy Shag, Curtain Bangs, Wavy Bob, Glossy Layers, Siren Waves, Pixie Cut, High Ponytail) paired with rich hair color tones (Midnight Raven, Dark Espresso, Chestnut, Honey Blonde, Platinum Frost, Cotton Candy Pink, Lilac Dream, Vibrant Copper).
- **Cosmetics & Makeup**: Custom iris eye colors, cheek blush warmth, dewy lip tints, and eyeliner styles (from natural bare to sharp alt wings and editorial red lip).
- **Full Persistence**: Seamlessly saves to `SaveSlot` and `localStorage`, preserving player customizations across reloads.

  - **Bottoms**: Washed blue straight-leg denim, high-waisted pleated black skater skirt, vintage light-wash mom jeans, Scottish red and black tartan punk mini with silver safety pin, tailored charcoal wide-leg slacks.
  - **Shoes**: Scuffed skate sneakers, classic black canvas low-tops with drawn red heart, chunky 2-inch platform Doc combat boots, pointed black leather ankle booties, champagne metallic strappy heels.
  - **Accessories**: Bulky matte black over-ear noise-canceling headphones resting around the neck, black velvet ribbon choker with silver crescent moon charm, dangling handcrafted silver safety-pin earrings on both ears, delicate gold chain with baroque freshwater pearl pendant.
- **Seamless Integration**: Live in both **Visual Novel scenes** and the **Vanity Dressing Room mirror**!
